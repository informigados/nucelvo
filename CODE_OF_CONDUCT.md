# 🤝 Code of conduct

Nucelvo welcomes users and contributors of all backgrounds, identities, abilities, and experience levels. This code applies to project spaces, including issues, pull requests, and their comments.

## Community expectations

- Discuss ideas and evidence respectfully. Give actionable feedback and assume people may be new to the project.
- Respect personal boundaries and protect other people's private information.
- Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, threats, and repeated unwanted contact are unacceptable.

## Reporting and moderation

Use the menu on the relevant issue, pull request, or comment to report it to repository administrators when GitHub offers that option. You can also report abuse through [GitHub's reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam). Include links to the content and enough context for review. Keep personal information and conduct reports out of public issues.

Maintainers review reports, limit sharing to the people needed to handle them, and take action proportionate to the behavior. Actions may include a warning, removing content, restricting participation, or blocking an account from the project.
